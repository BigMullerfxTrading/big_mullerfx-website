ABOUT THE BRAND

Trading With Purpose

The goal is to wait for quality opportunities, follow a defined trading plan, and avoid emotional decisions driven by fear, greed, or FOMO.

THE TRADING MODEL

My Trading Approach

A structured process for analysing the market and waiting for confirmation before execution.

01 / BIAS

Market Direction

Analyse higher timeframes to identify the broader market direction and important external liquidity levels.

02 / STRUCTURE

Market Structure

Study trends, Break of Structure (BOS), and Change of Character (CHOCH) to understand potential shifts in price.

03 / ZONES

Supply & Demand

Use supply and demand zones, support and resistance, and Fair Value Gaps as confluences to strengthen price action setups.

04 / CONFIRMATION

Candle-Close Confirmation

Wait for the candle to close and confirm the setup before considering an entry.

05 / LIQUIDITY

Liquidity Analysis

Monitor internal and external liquidity around important market highs, lows, and selected points of interest.

06 / RISK

Risk Management

Plan entries, stop losses, and targets before execution. Aim for opportunities offering at least a 1:3 risk-to-reward ratio when the setup supports it.
